Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 29, 2026

Gatherly (“we,” “us,” or “our”) operates the Gatherly service available at trygatherly.com (the “Service”). This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, and share information when you use the Service.

Information we collect

  • Account information. When you create an account, we collect your name, email address, phone number, and a password (stored using secure hashing—we do not store your password in plain text).
  • Contacts and social graph. We store contacts you add, friend requests, and friendships so you can coordinate plans with people you know.
  • Availability and plans. We store your weekly availability, plans, hangouts, responses, and related scheduling data you submit through the Service.
  • Messages. We store messages and optional images you send in hangout or plan conversations.
  • Notifications. If you enable browser push notifications, we store push subscription data (such as endpoint and encryption keys) needed to deliver notifications to your device. We may send email through our email provider for account-related messages (for example, password reset).
  • Preferences. We store settings such as your timezone, reminder preferences, and similar choices you make in the app.
  • Technical data. Like most web services, our servers receive standard technical information (for example, IP address, browser type, and timestamps) when you use the Service. We use sessions/cookies as needed for login and security.

How we use information

We use the information above to provide, maintain, and improve the Service; authenticate you; send transactional emails and push notifications you request; coordinate plans and availability among you and your friends; prevent abuse; and comply with law.

AI features

Some features may use third-party AI models to generate suggestions or assist with in-app tasks. When you use those features, relevant content you provide (for example, messages or prompts) may be sent to our AI provider to generate a response. Do not submit sensitive information you do not want processed by an AI provider.

How we share information

  • Other users. Information you choose to share through the Service (for example, availability visible to friends, plan details, or hangout messages) is visible to the users involved, as designed in the product.
  • Service providers. We use trusted vendors to host the Service, send email, and provide AI capabilities. They process information on our behalf under appropriate safeguards.
  • Legal and safety. We may disclose information if required by law, or if we believe disclosure is necessary to protect rights, safety, or security.

We do not sell your personal information.

Retention

We retain information for as long as your account is active and as needed to provide the Service, unless a longer period is required or permitted by law. You may request deletion as described below.

Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete personal information, or to object to or restrict certain processing. To make a request, contact us using the email address below. You can stop push notifications from your browser or device settings.

Children

The Service is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post the updated version on this page and update the “Last updated” date.

Contact

For questions or privacy requests regarding this policy, contact us at privacy@trygatherly.com.
