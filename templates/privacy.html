Gatherly Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 29, 2026

Gatherly ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding your data.

Information We Collect

When you use Gatherly, we collect the following information:

  • Phone number — used to verify your identity and create your account
  • Name — displayed to your friends within the app
  • Profile photo — optionally uploaded by you and shown to your friends
  • Availability — the time slots you mark as free each week
  • Plans and messages — hangout plans you create or are invited to, and messages sent within those plans
  • Friend connections — the list of people you've connected with on Gatherly
  • Device token — used to send you push notifications

How We Use Your Information

We use your information solely to provide the Gatherly service:

  • To authenticate your account via SMS verification
  • To show your availability to your friends so you can coordinate hangouts
  • To create and manage plans between friends
  • To send you push notifications about friend requests, plans, and RSVPs

We do not sell your personal information. We do not use your data for advertising purposes.

Third-Party Services

Gatherly uses the following third-party services to operate:

  • Twilio — for SMS verification of your phone number. Twilio Privacy Policy
  • Google Firebase — for data storage and push notifications. Firebase Privacy Policy

Data Sharing

Your information is only shared with people you choose to connect with on Gatherly. Specifically:

  • Your name, profile photo, and availability are visible to your Gatherly friends
  • Your phone number is never displayed to other users
  • Your data is never sold or shared with advertisers or third-party marketers

Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. When you delete your account through the app, we permanently delete your profile, availability, hosted plans, and all associated data. Some residual data may remain in backups for up to 30 days before being fully purged.

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Delete your account and all associated data at any time via the app's Profile screen
  • Contact us with any privacy concerns

Children's Privacy

Gatherly is not intended for users under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes through the app. Continued use of Gatherly after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
