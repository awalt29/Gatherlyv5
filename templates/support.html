Frequently Asked Questions

How do I add friends?

Search for friends by name in the Friends tab and send a request.

Why am I not receiving my verification code?

Make sure you entered your phone number correctly with the country code. Check your SMS messages and try again.

How do I set my availability?

Tap the grid cells on the home screen to mark when you're free.

How do I create a plan?

Long press on a time slot where you and friends overlap, or tap the + button on the Plans tab.
